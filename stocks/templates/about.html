About StockUp

StockUp is a team-based fantasy investment platform with real-time stock data.

What Can You Do on StockUp?


Team Up

Join teams, compete against other teams, and look at the leaderboards.

Invest

Invest in the market using real-time stock data from Yahoo Finance.

Predict

Get predictions for stock prices 30 days into the future.

Why Use StockUp?

StockUp is a fun and social way to practice investment skills without having to invest real money.
